# AfriMart Sales Dashboard
Tool Used: Excel

 Dataset: AfriMart_Sales_Dataset.xlsx (700 transactions, 10 countries, 7 products)
## Key Findings
## Insight 1 — Revenue Concentration
Rice drives 58.8% of revenue at just 15.56% margin. There is a need to diversify growth toward higher-margin products such as Palm Oil.

## Insight 2 — 2026 Revenue Decline
Revenue fell by 9.8% in 2026, driven mainly by a 16.1% decline in Rice profit margin; there is a need to investigate weaker categories while sustaining Palm Oil growth.

## Insight 3 — Nigeria Margin Gap
Nigeria generates 15.5% of revenue but only at 18.65% margin, driven partly by Rice's 70.3% revenue share. Reviewing product mix and costs is necessary to improve profit margin.
